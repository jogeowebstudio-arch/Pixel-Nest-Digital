how it happened

Seven years, in five moments

August 2019

A queue at Kavya Book House

Ananya was buying the last copy of a novel. Aarav had come for the same one. She let him have it on the condition that he returned it — which took him four months, and one very deliberate delay.

March 2021

Two cities, one call

Bengaluru and Kochi, six hundred kilometres, and a standing nine o'clock phone call that neither of them missed for two years.

November 2023

Meeting the families

Onam sadhya at the George house. Aarav ate four servings of payasam out of sheer nerves. Ananya's grandmother approved of him on that basis alone.

14 January 2026

The question, at Fort Kochi
